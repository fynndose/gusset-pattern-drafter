# Gusset – Pattern Drafter 1.0.0 beta 2

Gusset is a free program for drafting sewing patterns in 2D, with a 3D window where you can sew the patterns together and try them on an avatar. Everything runs on your own computer, and your patterns stay there.

This beta adds a place to make pictures of your garment, and makes tiled patterns easier to print and tape together. It is still a test version, so **please save your work often**.

**The easiest way to get it is the website:** [gussetdrafter.com](https://gussetdrafter.com/download) offers the right file for your computer, and you can also try Gusset in your browser with nothing to install.

## The first start

The builds are not signed yet, so Windows and macOS warn you the first time. [Install help](https://gussetdrafter.com/help) shows what to click:

- **Windows:** in "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** click **Done**, then System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ **Open Anyway**. If macOS says the app is damaged, run this in Terminal: `xattr -cr "/Applications/Gusset – Pattern Drafter.app"`
- **Linux:** allow the AppImage to run as a program. On Ubuntu 22.04 or newer you may need `libfuse2` (`libfuse2t64` on 24.04).

## What's new since 1.0.0 beta 1

### The Render workspace

**Render ▸ Render** now opens a workspace in the 2D window's place, with tabs at its foot to switch between the two. Its settings are in the Property Editor, in four panes: Image/Video, Camera, Light and Render.

- **Interactive Render** is a rough preview. Click the empty window to start it; the mouse in the picture moves the same camera as the 3D window. Stop it before a Final Render.
- **Final Render** is path traced by default, with soft shadows and light bouncing between the garment, the avatar and the floor. Pause, Resume and Stop are on the toolbar. Quick and Schematic are also on offer under Engine.
- **Turntable Images and Custom Views** make a series of pictures, saved as one zip. A turntable can add a GIF and a WebM video.
- **Lights:** Rectangle, Sphere, Directional, Spot and Dome, with five presets of our own. A rectangle or sphere shows in the picture when it stands in view.
- **Camera:** Field of View, clipping, and saved cameras.
- **Copy Current Image and Save Current Image.** In the desktop app, also **Show in Saved Folder**.
- A document keeps its render settings and custom views in its file.

**Getting the path tracer ready.** It prepares in the background from the moment the workspace opens, and you can keep working. This takes about 20 seconds on a mid-range graphics card, once each time the program starts. A bar shows it, with a Cancel button. If it fails or takes more than two minutes, Gusset uses Quick instead and says so.

**Without a graphics card,** Final Render uses Quick. Choosing Path Traced first tells you what it will cost for your picture.

### Quality Render and Schematic Render

Both are switches at the top of the 3D window's display buttons, off at every start.

- **Quality Render** draws the 3D window with soft shadows and a soft reflected room. It is a little slower.
- **Schematic Render** draws the garment as a line picture on white, for a working sketch. Its panel sets the thickness of the silhouette, seam, internal and topstitch lines, the line color and the garment's fill. **File ▸ Snapshot ▸ 3D Window** saves it, and its box previews every view.

### Print Layout

- **It opens trimmed:** the pattern as it will look once the sheets are cut and taped, with the margins gone and thin lines where the sheets meet. **Paper View**, on the bar, shows the whole sheets.
- **The Cut-off margin** runs from 0 to 30 mm.
- **New layouts have Overlap 0,** so sheets butt edge to edge. A file keeps the overlap it has.
- **Printed sheets** have a dashed cut line, and two small triangles on every edge that meets another sheet. Laid together, they make a diamond.
- **The first sheet has a 50 mm square** to measure.

### Updates

- **Gusset now asks the website whether a newer version is out,** and only published versions are listed there. Versions still download from GitHub.
- **A check that cannot be made is simply no update:** it no longer shows an error.

### Fixed

- **In a browser, Print opened an empty print window first,** and the right one only after you cancelled that. It now opens one window, with the pages in it.

## Known issues

- **The path tracer prepares again at every start of the program.** We have not found a way to keep the prepared result between starts.
- **Without a graphics card,** Path Traced may stop the program answering for up to about a minute while it prepares, and a large picture takes many minutes.
- **The Studio High Contrast light preset is much darker path traced** than in Quick. Burn Value brightens it.
- **A path-traced picture can show a horizon line** where the floor meets the background.
- **A saved turntable video needs the Render tab in front** while it is written.
- **The simulation is a proof of concept.** It shows roughly how a garment hangs. It is not accurate enough to judge fit.
- **The fabric presets, the size run and parts of the skirt block are placeholder values,** to be replaced by real ones.
- **DXF output has not been tried on a cutter.**
- **Printing true to size depends on printing at 100 %.** Measure the 50 mm square on the first sheet.
- The project-file import is known for files written in 2026 only.
- On Apple-chip Macs, a fold line at 270° settles a little further from its target than elsewhere.
