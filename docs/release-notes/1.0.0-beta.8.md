# Gusset – Pattern Drafter 1.0.0 beta 8: the Public Beta

Gusset is a free program for drafting sewing patterns in 2D, with a 3D window where you can sew the patterns together and try them on an avatar. Everything runs on your own computer, and your patterns stay there.

This is the first beta for everyone. **Everything you can see with the default settings works**: tools that are not ready yet are hidden. It is still a test version, so **please save your work often**, and tell us what you find.

**The easiest way to get it is the website:** [gussetdrafter.com](https://gussetdrafter.com/download) offers the right file for your computer, and you can also try Gusset in your browser with nothing to install.

## The first start

The builds are not signed yet, so Windows and macOS warn you the first time. [Install help](https://gussetdrafter.com/help) shows what to click, with pictures:

- **Windows:** in "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. The installer installs for you alone and asks for no administrator rights.
- **Mac:** open the `.dmg` and drag Gusset to Applications. At the first start macOS says it cannot check the app: click **Done**, then open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway** near the bottom. If macOS says the app is damaged, run this in Terminal: `xattr -cr "/Applications/Gusset – Pattern Drafter.app"`
- **Linux:** make the AppImage executable (its Properties ▸ Permissions, or `chmod +x` on the file), then double-click it. On Ubuntu 22.04 or newer you may need `libfuse2` (`libfuse2t64` on 24.04).

At the first start a welcome box shows a few things to try. Help ▸ Welcome… brings it back.

## If you tested a beta before

- **Your settings, library and unsaved work stay where they were.** Files from every earlier beta open in beta 8.
- **Files saved in beta 8 don't open in beta 7 or older.** The file format has a new version, for the new fabric, seam and tack settings below. Keep a copy saved by your old beta if you need to go back to it.
- **New patterns now stand where you draw them.** The 2D window's horizontal axis is the floor in 3D: a pattern drawn above the line stands above the floor, and New from Starter Pattern and File ▸ New Block… come in at body height. Patterns already in a file keep their places in 3D.
- **There are 116 arrangement points on the avatars** (50 before). The 50 earlier ones keep their names; the hip points moved up to the hips and the neck points to the base of the neck, so a garment placed on those may start a little differently.
- **Pattern names and grain lines are hidden at the start.** Switch them on in the 2D window's display button.
- **Show Tools That Are Not Built Yet now starts off**, so you see only what works. If you switched it on before, it stays on (Settings ▸ Show Tools That Are Not Built Yet).
- **Beta 1 to beta 7 tell you about this version** when they start, with an **Update available** button at the top right. You can also choose Help ▸ Check for Updates….
- **The 0.9.0 betas do not.** Download this version once by hand. If you imported project files with a 0.9.0 beta, their grainlines may be turned by 90°: choose **File ▸ Import ▸ Re-read Grainlines from Original File…** and pick the original `.zprj` or `.zpac`.

## What's new since 1.0.0 beta 7

### The 2D window

- **The avatar's outline stands on the floor line** behind your patterns, as a soft grey shape, from the front by default (Off, Back and Side in the 2D window's display button). Edit Avatar Outline is gone: it is always where the avatar really is.
- **A pattern being moved catches the others**: its corners and edges snap to another pattern's corners, outline or seam allowance line, with a small square at the catch.
- **Add Point catches level with the middle of another pattern's line**, with a dashed line to it.
- **Trace as a Pattern can close along internal lines**: select outline lines and the internal lines that run between them.
- **Grading a corner moves only that corner.** A click on a line with Edit Grading picks both its ends. Slash & Spread now carries the grading along.
- **A grain line stays inside its pattern**, however you move, turn or reshape it.
- **New rows on an outline point's right-click**: Add Dart with Fullness, and Perpendicular Pattern Corner.
- **Base Dart** (2D ▸ Create) draws a dart as a base line, a guide that is neither cut nor sewn.
- **The pattern's right-click menu has tabs**: All, Edit, Pattern, Line and Arrange, under its search field.
- **Assign to New Fabric** gives the selected patterns a new fabric of their own, copied from the first one's.
- The position readout no longer jumps as the numbers change.

### Sewing

- **A seam selected with Edit Sewing has its own settings** in the Property Editor: a Name, Tension (the seam drawn in by a share), Set Sublayer (one side lies under the other), Topstitch and Puckering along it, and how it shows on the cloth (a fine dark line by default).
- **Rips**: Seamline Rip leaves a stretch of a seam open in the simulation; Edit Rip moves or deletes it. The seam itself stays.
- **Set Sublayer** is also a tool in the 2D menu: click the pattern that lies under, then the one above it.
- **Seam Taping** (the Property Editor's Selected Line) keeps a line at its own length on the cloth.
- **Shirring** gathers the cloth along an elastic line in small folds.
- Clicking a long seam no longer misses it.

### 3D and the simulation

- **Pulling the cloth can no longer push it into the avatar or through it.** It slides round the body towards the mouse instead.
- **The fabric's Detail rows**: stretch, shear and bending in each direction, internal damping and friction (Property Editor ▸ Physical Property ▸ Detail).
- **Each fabric has a Front, Back and Side** in its Material, and **a fabric can have a texture**: a picture repeated along each pattern's grain line, moved and turned with Edit Texture.
- **Tacks**: before you click, a dot shows where the tack will land, and a line follows the mouse to the second place. A tack has its own settings: Name, Activate, and Thread Length (it hangs slack up to that length).
- **The blue dot in the middle of Select/Move's handles**, dragged onto another pattern, lays the selected patterns on it, facing as that cloth faces.
- **Steam** shrinks or relaxes the cloth where you brush it. **Glue Brush** glues the shape you paint to the layer under it.
- **Select Mesh** (box, lasso or brush) picks cloth points, which you can then freeze.
- **A pattern's Pressure** pushes its cloth outwards or inwards.
- **Hi-Res Properties** has an extra collision thickness and the Simulation Quality.
- **1:1 View** shows the 3D window at true size where the view is centred.
- Press **A** over the 3D window for Select/Move.
- **Easier to find**: Avatar ▸ Measurement for the avatar tapes, 3D ▸ Garment Resolution, 3D ▸ Measurement for volumes, and a new **Fabric** menu.

### Render

- **Camera Properties** (the 3D background's right-click, or Settings ▸ Preferences) opens the camera's settings in the Render workspace.
- Seams show on the cloth as fine lines in renders too.

### Print, plot and export

- **The Plotter box has every line and information option**: punch marks, graphic frames, the shrinkage line, buffer spacing, buttons and buttonholes, the fabric's outline and a line of nesting information under the marker.
- **Nest All Patterns keeps looking for a shorter marker** until you press **Stop Nesting**, for up to 30 seconds.
- **DXF export** can leave out the seam allowance, and can write UTF-8 or Windows-1252 text.
- The BOM counts the buttons and buttonholes you placed.

### The library

- **Every library item** (fabric, button, buttonhole, topstitch, puckering, trim, zipper) has a **…** menu in the Object Browser: Duplicate, Rename, Export and Import as a file, and Delete.
- **A button catalogue**: the Button tab's Catalogue… has 12 ready-made buttons.
- **The Open Garment box** asks whether to open a library garment or add it to your project, and by how much to move it when added. File ▸ Add ▸ Project uses it too.
- A library item's right-click has Add to Workspace and, in the desktop app, Copy the Path and Show in Explorer for your own patterns.

### Settings and shortcuts

- **Settings ▸ User Settings… has pages**: User Interface, View Controls, Shortcuts, 2D, 3D and Other.
- **You can change the keyboard shortcuts** on the Shortcuts page. Help ▸ Keyboard Shortcuts… shows the keys you use now and has Reset to Default.
- **The magnifier beside the Library button searches every menu.**
- **Help ▸ Show Tool Hints** shows what the tool in your hand does.
- Display ▸ Window ▸ Object Browser folds and unfolds the Object Browser.

### History

- **Create History Group…** is on a pattern's right-click menu, in the 2D and the 3D window.

## Known issues

- **Files from this beta may not open in 1.0** if the file format changes before then. The format will be fixed at the release candidate. **Keep an export (DXF or PDF) of important work.**
- **While you pull the cloth, a long stretched edge can cut across the avatar for a moment.** It comes back round once you let go.
- **Sending to a plotter directly is hidden** until it has been tried on a real plotter. File ▸ Export ▸ Plotter (PLT) writes the file, which your plotter's own software can send.
- **Auto Sewing is not available yet.** Sew with the sewing tools.
- **The Stress Map's N/m and kPa and the Pressure Map are relative values**, from the simulation's own forces. Use them to compare places on a garment, not as measurements.
- **1:1 View** assumes the system's display scaling is 100 %. At another scaling it is that much larger or smaller.
- **A fabric's Back and Side** keep their Type and Texture Mapping, but these are not drawn yet.
- **Changing an early step does not yet update the later steps.** If you go back from the History panel, change something and keep the old steps as a branch, that branch stays exactly as it was.
- **Fold Arrangement turns the cloth as it hangs and does not simulate.** Press Simulate afterwards if you want the folded part to settle.
- **Split Off copies do not follow the original**, and going back in one History Group leaves sewing to patterns outside the group as it is.
- **How the cloth hung is kept only at Save States** and a few other places. Going to any other step says "This step's cloth was not kept: Re-drape or Simulate".
- **The history keeps at most 500 steps.** Older steps are dropped, but never Save States or the points where a branch starts.
- **The simulation is a proof of concept.** It shows roughly how a garment hangs. It is not accurate enough to judge fit.
- **The avatar's arm does not move,** so a sleeve pulled forward cannot go further than the arm inside it allows.
- **A Seamless Blocking section's edge follows the cloth's triangles,** not the block line exactly, and a blocked pattern stays one piece in Print Layout.
- **Re-drape** does not carry cloth that hangs more than about 15 cm from the body. Auto 3D Arrangement is not available yet.
- **Simulation Quality and your shortcuts are settings of your computer**, not of the file.
- **Adding a library garment or a project brings no avatar** with it.
- **Once patterns are graded, the base size cannot be changed.**
- **Symmetric sewing** works only on a Symmetric Pattern copy, and only when the pattern has exactly one. Zippers are not mirrored.
- **The path tracer gets ready again at every start**, about 20 seconds on a mid-range graphics card. Without a graphics card, Final Render uses Quick. On some cards a picture's size limit is only found by trying: if a render fails, try again.
- **In a browser**, Path Traced, a Particle Distance under 10 mm and pictures over 4096 pixels are the desktop app's only, as before.
- **The fabric presets, the size run and parts of the skirt block are placeholder values,** to be replaced by real ones.
- **DXF output has not been tried on a cutter.** Printing true to size depends on printing at 100 %: measure the 50 mm square on the first sheet.
- The project-file import is known for files written in 2026 only, and leaves out trims, zippers, topstitches, textures and the avatar.
