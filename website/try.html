Try Gusset in your browser

It is the whole program, with nothing to install. About 5 MB loads once, and your patterns stay on your computer.

Open GussetOpens in this tab

Before you start

  • Use a computer with a mouse and a current Chrome, Edge or Firefox. A phone is too small for it. The 3D window needs WebGL, which almost every computer has.
  • Save with File, Save. Your pattern goes to your downloads as a .pdraft file, which you open again with File, Open. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Unsaved work is kept in this browser and offered back at the next start. Clearing your browser's data removes it, so save to a file for anything you want to keep.
  • A few keys belong to the browser, such as Ctrl+N and Ctrl+W. The downloaded program has them too, and opens .pdraft files with a double-click.
