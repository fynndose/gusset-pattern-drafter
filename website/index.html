Draft your own sewing patterns.

Gusset is a free pattern-drafting program. Draw the pattern in 2D, sew the pieces together on an avatar in 3D, and print it true to size.

Download GussetWindows, Mac, Linux. Free. Try it in your browserNothing to install

Who it is for

People who sew

Draft or alter your own patterns and print them at home, tiled over ordinary sheets of paper.

Pattern-making students

Learn with a tool that costs nothing, and take apart how every line was made.

Developers

Build on a small code base for pattern geometry, written in plain JavaScript.

Free and open source

Gusset costs nothing and needs no account. It runs on your own computer, and your patterns stay there.

The source code is open under the GNU General Public License, so anyone can read it, change it and pass it on.
